# How long pieces of work took

**Question.** How long the epic's pieces took, to plan the rest. Stephen's
time is the constrained resource: it is linear.

**Method.** An agent's measure from commits and transcripts (2 Oct); send
times and decision counts inferred, sizes and build and merge times
observed.

**Found.**
- A builder's first commit lands 15 to 35 minutes after its brief is sent.
- With Stephen present: a small piece 45 minutes to 1.5 hours; a medium one
  1 to 2 hours; a spec 1.75 to 3 hours when its design is settled. The
  durable spec, sent with decisions still open, took about 39 hours of wall
  time and 4 reviews.
- Throughput was about one merged piece per 2 hours of his presence (about
  15 pieces in about 30 active hours). Running work in parallel didn't
  shorten his part.
- Decisions per piece: 1 to 2 small, 3 to 8 medium, 11 to 15 for an open
  design. So an item that needs a design talk gets one before its brief.
- Examples: the foundation, 32 files, about 1.5 hours, about 11 decisions;
  the bus, 26 files, about 1.1 hours, about 4; the publisher, 26 files, about
  50 minutes, about 3; the kit, 3 files, about 25 minutes.

**Resume comparison.** Not applicable.
